An exploded view The anatomy of the perfect screening

  1. I The film

    70mm

    Ten times the picture.

    Each IMAX 70mm frame runs sideways through the projector, fifteen perforations wide: about ten times the image area of standard 35mm.

  2. II The screen

    40m×28m

    The largest cinema screen in the world.

    Nine storeys of picture in the full 1.43:1 IMAX frame, filling your view from edge to edge and floor to ceiling.

  3. III The projector

    1.43:1

    The full IMAX frame.

    Our projectionists thread every print by hand. A rolling-loop movement holds each frame flat and perfectly still while the light passes through it.

  4. IV The sound

    12channels

    Sound from every direction.

    Speakers behind the screen, along the walls and overhead place every sound exactly where it happens.

  5. V The seats

    420seats

    No bad seat in the house.

    Steep stadium rows in hand-stitched velvet. Every seat sees the whole screen, top to bottom, clear over the row in front.
